# Design & Presentation Tips

## Color Scheme
- **Primary:** Blue/Teal (#2563eb to #0891b2)
- **Secondary:** Purple/Pink accent (#9333ea)
- **Background:** White with subtle gradients

## Timing
- **Total:** 10-12 minutes presentation
- ~1 minute per slide
- Leave time for Q&A

## Slide Breakdown
- Slides 1-3: Problem & concept (2 min)
- Slides 4-5: Solution overview (2 min)
- Slides 6-7: Examples & demo (3 min)
- Slides 8-10: Tech, market & CTA (2-3 min)

## Key Visuals to Include
1. Gradient logo and branding
2. Termite mound & building comparison
3. Nature photography (gecko, whale, lotus)
4. App interface/dashboard screenshots
5. 3D model renderings
6. Market charts and graphs

## Presentation Flow
1. **Hook:** Start with the problem (months vs minutes)
2. **Tell a Story:** Use termite mound as your star example
3. **Show Value:** Market opportunity is huge
4. **Call to Action:** Clear ask (beta access, investment, partnership)

## Pro Tips
- Keep bullet points minimal (5-6 words max)
- Use high-quality nature photography
- One main idea per slide
- Lots of white space
- Consistent branding throughout
- Practice transitions smoothly
- Have a live demo ready as backup slide

# Speaker Notes Structure

**Slide 1 (30 sec):** Introduce yourself and project. Set the tone with excitement about nature-inspired innovation.

**Slide 2 (1 min):** Paint the problem. Tell a story about how long R&D takes. Why is this frustrating?

**Slide 3 (1 min):** Explain biomimicry concept briefly. Give 2-3 relatable examples (Velcro, trains, wings).

**Slide 4 (1 min):** Introduce the solution. Show the three-step workflow. Emphasize the speed: months → minutes.

**Slide 5 (1 min):** Walk through each step. This is your core product demo.

**Slide 6 (1.5 min):** Your STAR example. Termite mounds are fascinating and relatable. Show the cooling system visually.

**Slide 7 (1.5 min):** Show 3 more solutions quickly. Emphasize the breadth of what's possible.

**Slide 8 (1 min):** Tech stack shows you built this seriously. Market size shows opportunity is real.

**Slide 9 (1 min):** Why you'll win. Competitive advantages. Vision statement.

**Slide 10 (1 min):** Clear CTA. What do you want from the audience? Investment? Users? Partnerships?
